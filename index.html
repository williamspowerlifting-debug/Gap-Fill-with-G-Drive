📖 How to Use

Create a listening exercise in under 2 minutes.

1. Upload a video

Three ways to add your video:

  • Drag & drop or click — upload a local file (MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI)
  • Paste a direct .mp4 link — any publicly accessible video URL
  • Google Drive link — paste a Drive share link. The file must be shared as "Anyone with the link can view". Import happens server-side and may take 1–3 minutes for large files.

2. Wait for transcript

The transcript appears automatically — no button to click.

3. Edit gaps

  • Click any word to make it a gap (turns yellow).
  • Click a yellow word to remove the gap.
  • "✏️ Edit Text" button — click directly in the transcript box to fix mistakes. Your gaps are preserved.

4. Click "Make it happen"

AI fills in extra gaps based on the level you choose. Your manual selections are kept.

5. Share with students

  • Preview — See what students will see.
  • Student Link — Copy and send. No signup needed.

How students use it

  1. The video plays the first sentence automatically.
  2. They type the missing words — they can change their answers as many times as needed.
  3. They can replay each sentence as many times as they want.
  4. When ready, they click ✅ Submit — green ✓ for correct, red ✗ for wrong (with the correct answer shown).
  5. The next sentence loads automatically after 6 seconds, or press Next to skip ahead immediately.
  6. At the end, they see their score.
